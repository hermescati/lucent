The Android navigation bar matches iOS 27 in more of its looks, measured against the simulator: an opaque bar (`headerTransparent: false`) sits at the top and follows a collapsing large title, in the system background or `headerStyle.backgroundColor`, and a coloured one sets the status bar's icons to read on it; `headerTintColor` colours the title while the glass buttons keep the text colour; bar glyphs come out SF Symbols' size; a `prominent` or `done` button fills with its own `tintColor`; the back button never cuts its title short, giving way to "Back" and then the chevron, and draws `generic` as the chevron alone; a search field that hides when scrolling starts hidden under the title; the bar's search button shares the trailing buttons' capsule, and `integratedCentered` beside a toolbar is that button; focused, a search field in the bar rises into the row beside a close button, as on iOS; a title view gets a bare bar; the inline title sits beside the back button when it doesn't fit centred; the titles blur as they cross-fade; the scroll edge washes and blurs as iOS's default, soft and hard edges do; and on a phone with a camera cutout in the top's middle, the bar starts below it. A screen whose content scrolls less than its large title's height no longer freezes with the title half-collapsed, its taps lost to a snap that kept trying to scroll past the content's end: `TabScrollView` gives such content the room iOS gives it to collapse the title, and the snap never aims past the end. Back on a stack's first screen now goes on to the system instead of raising a navigation error. Inside `NativeTabs`, a bottom toolbar sits under the tab bar, as on iOS 27, showing between its circles once it minimizes; through a push or a pop the bottom toolbar's buttons stay where they stand and blur and fade with their screen, as the bar's do, instead of sliding with the page; a `hidden` tab bar fades out and back in as iOS's does, instead of vanishing and popping back in; and the tab bar minimizes only for the person's own scrolling, not for content starting scrolled past a hidden search field. A bar button's badge is iOS's size and stands out over the capsule's corner instead of being clipped by its glass, and a bare `Stack.Toolbar.View` gets a bar button's width around it. Search placed in the navigation bar follows iOS 27 more closely: a focused field rises a point past the row and settles back, and the close button drops it back on a critically damped spring; and the magnifier among the bar's items now opens the field in the bar's row, its capsule stretching left into the field and the close button budding off its end, closing back into the magnifier, instead of opening the field at the bottom. A field in a tab goes under the title, or the magnifier among the bar's items, even with the tab bar hidden; a field that scrolls away is a flat grey pill at rest; the clear button is iOS's; and the cursor takes the search bar's tint or the system blue.
