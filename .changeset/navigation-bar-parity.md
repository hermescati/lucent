The Android navigation bar matches iOS 27 in more of its looks, measured against the simulator: an opaque bar (`headerTransparent: false`) sits at the top and follows a collapsing large title, in the system background or `headerStyle.backgroundColor`, and a coloured one sets the status bar's icons to read on it; `headerTintColor` colours the title while the glass buttons keep the text colour; bar glyphs come out SF Symbols' size; a `prominent` or `done` button fills with its own `tintColor`; the back button never cuts its title short, giving way to "Back" and then the chevron, and draws `generic` as the chevron alone; a search field that hides when scrolling starts hidden under the title; the bar's search button shares the trailing buttons' capsule, and `integratedCentered` beside a toolbar is that button; focused, a search field in the bar rises into the row beside a close button, as on iOS; a title view gets a bare bar; the inline title sits beside the back button when it doesn't fit centred; the titles blur as they cross-fade; the scroll edge washes and blurs as iOS's default, soft and hard edges do; and on a phone with a camera cutout in the top's middle, the bar starts below it. Back on a stack's first screen now goes on to the system instead of raising a navigation error.
