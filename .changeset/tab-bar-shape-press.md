On Android, the tab bar's search circle, its minimized bar and its search field and back button are pressed as every other glass button is, as iOS 27's are: on the same spring, a little past full size on the way in and wobbling back on release, where they swelled stiffly and settled without give. Held, the search circle leans toward the finger on it and its pull trails a dragged finger rather than jumping with it, and the minimized bar lights up as a pressed button does, where it barely brightened.
