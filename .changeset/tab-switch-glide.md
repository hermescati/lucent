On Android, a tapped tab's lens glides as iOS 27's does: it eases off, stretches with its speed and springs back, and eases into its tab over a long tail by about 250ms. While it travels it deforms lopsided, its top flattened and its trailing end drawn out toward its tip while its leading end stays round, then rounds up as it stops, its bottom bulging out of the bar. It's outlined as a held lens is, with a colour fringe, and the tabs under it keep their size. Let go mid-drag, the lens carries a little past its tab and back, stays lifted a moment, then fills while still spread wide and gathers to its size, and the bar stays swollen until it lands. Older Android's flat glass no longer redraws every frame while lifted.
