Add `List`, `ListItem` and `FieldGroup`, with `@expo/ui`'s universal API plus `listStyle`, a row's `detail`, `accessory`, `destructive`, `disabled` and `swipeActions`. On iOS they're SwiftUI's `List` and `Form`; on Android Lucent draws iOS 27's: inset grouped cards or plain rows, section headers and footers, values with a chevron or a checkmark, swipe actions on capsule buttons, and pull to refresh with iOS's spinner, scrolling under the navigation bar and tab bar as their own scroll views do.
