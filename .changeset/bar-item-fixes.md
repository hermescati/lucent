'expo-lucent': patch

On Android, a navigation bar button's badge rides its icon as the capsule grows and leans under a press, as iOS's does, where it stayed put. A selected button's fill is no longer cut off at its capsule's edge, at rest or pressed. A selected icon among others is filled 41pt wide, centred on its glyph, as iOS 27 fills it, where the fill was 38pt and sat at the item's start; a lone selected icon's circle is centred too. The fill keeps as far from the capsule's end as from its top and bottom. Pressing one button of several on a capsule lights that button, fading along the capsule, rather than the whole capsule.
