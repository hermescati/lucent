Animate an icon button joining or leaving its neighbours' capsule when `separateBackground` changes, as the tab bar's search circle melts in and splits off: joining, its own capsule shrinks into the shared one's end as that grows to take its icon; leaving, it grows back out of that end, necked to it halfway, as the shared one closes. Both run on the tab bar's split spring.
