On Android, pressed glass follows a finger dragged off it, as iOS 27's does: it grows longer toward the finger and narrower across, its middle moving toward it, more the further the finger goes (measured on the simulator, up to 15% longer with the finger about 580pt away), and springs back when let go. Navigation bar and toolbar capsules, their contents with them, and pressable glass of every kind. It's stretched as a whole, as iOS's is: a circle pulls into an ellipse and a text button keeps its flat top and bottom, where the shape was drawn anew at its new size, squarish or as a round bubble. What's on it stretches with it, as iOS's icons do, where an icon kept its size poked out of the narrowed glass and was cut off. The press's light fills the whole stretched shape, where it stayed inside the button's resting outline and left a darker band at each end.
