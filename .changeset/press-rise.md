On Android, pressing the selected tab lifts its lens on iOS 27's curve, however long it's held: it rises without overshooting or sagging back, to iOS's height. A quick tap carries on up to three quarters of that lift and drops back from the same moment whether it was let go at 50 or 200ms, and a released lens falls back to the bar as quickly as iOS's does.
