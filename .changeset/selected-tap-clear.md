On Android, pressing or tapping the selected tab lifts its lens as iOS 27's does: the same bubble as a drag lifts, a capsule of clear glass magnifying the tab, popping up and settling back into its pill as one smooth swell, its width keeping to its height rather than trailing it. A tap lifts it fully and lets it settle about 250ms after touch-down however short it was, springing on from its rise, and each of a run of quick taps plays it through. The bar's glass stays lit and the lens clear while it's up, where letting go dimmed it at once, the magnified tab settles into the one under it rather than showing over it twice, and the bar swells about 3pt, relaxing over about 200ms.
