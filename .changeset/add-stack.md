Add `Stack`, with the same API as expo-router's. On iOS it is expo-router's `Stack`; on Android, Lucent draws iOS 26's navigation bar: a glass back button, `Stack.Toolbar` buttons grouped onto glass capsules in the bar and in the bottom toolbar (which floats above the tab bar and the keyboard), the large title collapsing into the bar on scroll, and the scroll edge blurring content under it. Screens push with iOS's slide and swipe back from anywhere, as on iOS 26. `Stack.Toolbar.Menu` opens iOS 26's glass menu, with sections, checkmarks and submenus. `Stack.Toolbar.Badge` badges bar buttons, and `Stack.SearchBar` draws iOS 26's search field, at the bottom, in a `Stack.Toolbar.SearchBarSlot`, or in the navigation bar, or as a magnifier button with `placement="integratedButton"`. Holding the back button opens a menu of the screens further back, and `scrollEdgeEffects: { top: 'hard' }` gives the hard scroll edge. `Stack.Toolbar.Button`, `Stack.Toolbar.Icon`, `Stack.Toolbar.Menu` and `Stack.Toolbar.MenuAction` gain `md` for a Material Symbol on Android. `TabScrollView` insets its content under the bar and drives it; `TabScrollView` also ends its content above a bottom toolbar. `useHeaderScroll()`, `useHeaderInset()` and `useToolbarInset()` do the same for other scroll views.
