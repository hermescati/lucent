# Changelog

All notable changes to Lucent are listed here. Each release's entries come from the changesets merged since the last one (see [.changeset](.changeset/README.md)).

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 59afd4b: Add `Button`, with the same API as `@expo/ui`'s plus `glass` and `glassProminent` variants, `icon`, `size`, `destructive` and `tintColor`. On iOS it renders SwiftUI's button; on Android, Lucent draws iOS's capsule buttons in each style, with real glass for the glass ones. `useButtonColor()` gives custom `children` the label colour. The theme gains `buttonFill`, `buttonDestructive` and `buttonDisabled`.
- 59afd4b: Add `ContentUnavailableView`: an empty state with a `title`, `sf` (`md` on Android), `description` and `actions`, centred in the space it fills. On iOS it renders SwiftUI's `ContentUnavailableView`; on Android, Lucent draws iOS's grey symbol, bold title and secondary description.
- 59afd4b: Add `ProgressView`: a spinner, or a bar for a `value` from 0 to 1, with a `label` and `tintColor`. On iOS it renders SwiftUI's `ProgressView`; on Android, Lucent draws iOS's grey spinner, turning a spoke at a time, and its thin capsule bar.
- 59afd4b: Add `Stack`, with the same API as expo-router's. On iOS it is expo-router's `Stack`; on Android, Lucent draws iOS 26's navigation bar: a glass back button, `Stack.Toolbar` buttons grouped onto glass capsules in the bar and in the bottom toolbar (which floats above the tab bar and the keyboard), the large title collapsing into the bar on scroll, and the scroll edge blurring content under it. Screens push with iOS's slide and swipe back from anywhere, as on iOS 26. `Stack.Toolbar.Menu` opens iOS 26's glass menu, with sections, checkmarks and submenus. `Stack.Toolbar.Badge` badges bar buttons, and `Stack.SearchBar` draws iOS 26's search field, at the bottom, in a `Stack.Toolbar.SearchBarSlot`, or in the navigation bar, or as a magnifier button with `placement="integratedButton"`. Holding the back button opens a menu of the screens further back, and `scrollEdgeEffects: { top: 'hard' }` gives the hard scroll edge. `Stack.Toolbar.Button`, `Stack.Toolbar.Icon`, `Stack.Toolbar.Menu` and `Stack.Toolbar.MenuAction` gain `md` for a Material Symbol on Android. `TabScrollView` insets its content under the bar and drives it; `TabScrollView` also ends its content above a bottom toolbar. `useHeaderScroll()`, `useHeaderInset()` and `useToolbarInset()` do the same for other scroll views.
- 59afd4b: Add `Stepper`: minus and plus buttons that step `value` between `min` and `max` by `step`, with an optional `label` that makes it a full-width row. On iOS it renders SwiftUI's `Stepper`; on Android, Lucent draws iOS 26's grey capsule, each side greying out at its end, stepping again while held.
- 59afd4b: `Stack` screens take `headerLargeTitleInline`: with `headerLargeTitle`, the large title sits on the bar's row beside its buttons, as in Photos › Collections, rather than under them. On iOS it's UIKit's `largeTitleDisplayMode = .inline` (iOS 17 and later), which expo-router can't ask for; Lucent sets it on the screen's navigation item. On Android Lucent draws it, from the simulator: 34pt bold, centred on the buttons, the bar an inline one's height, cross-fading to the inline title once content scrolls under the bar. As in UIKit, only a screen without a back button takes it; one with keeps the large title under the row.
- 1e34d4d: Add `List`, `ListItem` and `FieldGroup`, with `@expo/ui`'s universal API plus `listStyle`, a row's `detail`, `accessory` (a chevron, a checkmark or UIKit's ⓘ, with `onAccessoryPress`), `button`, `destructive`, `disabled` and `swipeActions`, a list's `editMode`, `selection` and `onSelectionChange`, `List.ForEach`'s `onDelete` and `onMove`, and a section's `headerProminence`, `headerAction` and `indexLabel`, and on Android an optional glass lens for the index (`indexLens`). On iOS they're SwiftUI's `List` and `Form`; on Android Lucent draws iOS 27's, measured against the simulator: inset grouped cards or plain rows, section headers and footers, pinned under the bar in a plain list, values with a chevron, a checkmark or an ⓘ, action rows, swipe actions on capsule buttons drawn as iOS draws them for the row, full swipes, edit mode with delete controls, reordering and multi-select, the section index, and pull to refresh with iOS's spinner, scrolling under the navigation bar and tab bar as their own scroll views do.
- 59afd4b: Move the tab bar, navigation bar and search bar to `expo-lucent/navigation`. `NativeTabs`, `TabScrollView`, `TabScrollMarker`, `useTabBarScroll`, `useTabBarInset`, `Stack`, `useHeaderScroll`, `useHeaderInset`, `useToolbarInset`, `SearchBar` and `useTabSearch` now import from there; glass, theming and the controls stay in `expo-lucent`. The navigation entry is the only one that needs expo-router, react-native-screens, react-native-safe-area-context and react-native-keyboard-controller, which are now optional peer dependencies, so an app that only wants glass and controls installs only what those need.

  To update, change the import of each of those names from `'expo-lucent'` to `'expo-lucent/navigation'`.

- 59afd4b: Draw `selected` on `Stack.Toolbar.Button` as iOS 27 does: a filled circle inside the button's capsule, or a pill around a label, in the button's tint or the system blue, with the glyph showing the glass through it.
- 59afd4b: Draw one solid fallback below Android 13 and with high-contrast text, in place of Android 12's blur and older Androids' flat glass. `GlassFallback` is now `'auto' | 'solid'`.
- 59afd4b: Paint the tab bar's solid fallback as One UI 9 paints its bar: an opaque surface, a lighter selected pill, a soft drop shadow and a hairline edge, the selected tab in the tint and the others grey. The bar keeps its size under a finger, and the selected pill keeps its resting shape, sliding from tab to tab on the glass bar's springs, with no lens lifting out of it. New colours `barFallbackPill`, `barFallbackShadow`, `barFallbackRim` and `tabInk`, and `useLucentTheme()` reports `solid`.
- 59afd4b: On Android, the stack pushes and pops with the swipe back's motion, iOS 26's: a pushed screen slides in from the right over the one it covers, which slides partway left and dims, with the rounded corners, shadow and bar buttons of the swipe, on iOS's push spring (fitted to recordings). The back button, the Back key and Android's back gesture pop the screen the same way in reverse. With predictive back on (`android.predictiveBackGestureEnabled`, Android 14 and later), the back gesture from either edge drives the slide as you drag, on the UI thread, and goes or stays as Android decides. Screens with an `animation` of their own keep react-native-screens' animation.

## 0.1.0

The first version of Lucent: iOS 26 Liquid Glass for Expo, on Android too. Each component copies an Expo API. On iOS it renders Expo's own component, and on Android Lucent draws an iOS-styled recreation.

### Glass

- `GlassView`, `GlassContainer`, `isLiquidGlassAvailable` and `isGlassEffectAPIAvailable`, with the same API as `expo-glass-effect`. On Android, Lucent draws the glass with its own refraction shader, matched to iOS in light and dark, with colour tints, the outline and the press physics.
- `GlassBackdrop`, which marks the content Android glass refracts. On iOS it's a plain `View`.

### Tab bar

- `NativeTabs`, with the same API as `expo-router/native-tabs`, including triggers, icons, labels, badges, vector icons and `NativeTabs.BottomAccessory`. On Android, Lucent draws the floating glass bar with the lens bubble, dragging between tabs, disabled tabs and minimize on scroll.
- On Android, the selected tab's pill takes the tab's width, so longer labels fit inside it, and in dark mode it sits a shade lighter than the bar.
- On Android, a search tab without an icon gets a magnifier, as on iOS, and the tabs paint the navigation theme's background behind screens that have none, so the glass always has something to refract.
- On iOS, `NativeTabs` takes `LucentProvider`'s `tintColor` when it isn't given one, so the accent reaches the tab bar and the native controls inside it.
- SVG tab icons.
- `TabScrollView`, `TabScrollMarker`, `useTabBarScroll` and `useTabBarInset`, for minimize on scroll and keeping content clear of the bar.

### Search bar

- `SearchBar`, with the same props as expo-router's `Stack.SearchBar`. On Android, the search tab morphs into the field, shows the clear button and rides the keyboard.
- `useTabSearch`, for reading the search text and state from any screen.

### Controls

- `Switch` and `Slider`, with the same API as `@expo/ui`.
- `SegmentedControl`, with the same API as `@expo/ui/community/segmented-control`.
- On Android, all three use a shared glass lens whose motion and bend are matched frame by frame to iOS.

### Theming

- `LucentProvider` and `useLucentTheme`, for the colour scheme, accent colour, glass opacity and colour overrides across the app.

### Docs and playground

- A docs page for each component, plus guides for theming and API design.
- The example app is a playground with a page for every component, where its props can be changed live and the code copied.
